  • Pennine Basin Carboniferous sandstones (Barnsley, Mexborough, Greenmoor suites; Clifton Rock) OPEN (not excluded in paper)
    U12 · Pennine Basin Carboniferous sandstones (Barnsley, Mexborough, Greenmoor suites; Clifton Rock)
    OPEN (not excluded in paper)

    Formation / basin: Carboniferous, Pennine Basin

    Tests: Zircon (Morton et al. 2021, Fig. 12i,j); Sr-Nd (Leng et al. 1999, Fig. 13); chrome spinel suites (Hallsworth & Chisholm 2000)

    Paper's conclusion: Not fully excluded. Barnsley/Mexborough/Greenmoor suites have Neoproterozoic and Early Palaeozoic zircon groups not seen in the Altar Stone. The Clifton suite is 'entirely of Laurentian aspect'; the Laurentian-source interpretation of the zircons 'does not exclude a potential source for the Altar Stone from the Carboniferous'. Sr-Nd: 'very little overlap'; 'an ORS provenance is more likely than a Carboniferous source'.

    Position: unmapped. Named in text; no coordinates

    Source: Pirrie et al. 2026 – Provenance discussion; supplementary data
